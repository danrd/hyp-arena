# Reader v12: a relevance filter before reading and an answer for every paper (plan, 2026-11-08)

Status: plan for the user's review; no code, no calls yet.

## Why (numbers of record: specs/reading-gold-2026-11-08.yaml, specs/balance-check-2026-11-08.yaml)

- 1 abstract in 10 that the search returns for a link holds an own finding on it (Argon pack 9, 200 pairs).
- On those, the reader keeps 38-48% (recall) and half of its records are not own findings (precision about 0.5);
  the direction is right whenever both have a finding (27 of 27).
- 64% of the lost key papers are not mentioned in the reader's reply at all: the skip is silent.

## Two changes

1. **Relevance filter before reading.**
   - Free rule: PubMed publication type. A review or editorial is passed on only when its abstract states a pooled or
     quantitative result (a number with an interval or p value), so narrative reviews drop out.
   - One short call per link (DeepSeek): the claim and, per paper, the title and the first 600 characters of the
     abstract; the answer for every PMID is "own result on this claim's outcome: yes / no / unsure". Only yes and unsure
     go to the reader. The answer must list every PMID (code checks it).
2. **Reader v12** (verify_claim/v12 = v9 + one change): the reply must contain one entry per PMID it was given, with
   finding for / against / none and, for for / against, the records as now. Code checks that every PMID is answered and
   asks again once for the missing ones; a skip becomes a visible "none" or a counted failure. Batches of at most 10 papers
   (as in the pilot). v9's other rules stay, so only these two things change.

## Evaluation (dev / holdout, fixed in a spec before any call)

- **Dev:** the 10 links of pack 9 (200 pairs with a per-paper gold). At most two prompt revisions, on dev numbers only.
  Measures: recall and precision of own findings, direction agreement, share of PMIDs answered, share filtered out
  and how many gold findings the filter drops.
- **Holdout:** the 30 pilot trials (102 links) on their own searches, scored against pack 8 (key papers on their link,
  link and trial Spearman), next to the original, second v9 and cross-link readings already computed. Run once.
- **Pass:** dev recall >= 0.65 and precision >= 0.65 with direction agreement kept, the filter drops at most 1 of the 21
  gold findings; holdout recall of key papers at least 1.5x the original (0.16) and link Spearman up by more than the
  reader noise (0.03).
- **Then** both pools in full and R2 / R3 under the 0.03 rule, as for every change of the records; cross-link reading is
  re-tested on top of v12 only if it passes.
