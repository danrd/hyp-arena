# ITP Archive Findings

I investigated the Internet Archive for the NIA Interventions Testing Program (ITP) pages listing compounds proposed or in testing, and whether they provided any rationale.

## Archived URLs and Dates

Several archived pages exist for the ITP program, including:
- `http://www.nia.nih.gov/research/dab/interventions-testing-program-itp` (First found around 2012-01-27)
- `http://www.nia.nih.gov/research/dab/interventions-testing-program-itp/compounds-testing` (Captured from 2012 to 2014)

## Compounds and Rationales

I checked the `compounds-testing` page which contains tables of compounds in testing.

* The pages **do not give a rationale** per compound. They only provide tables containing:
  - `Compound` (e.g., Aspirin, NDGA, Rapamycin, Resveratrol, Metformin, etc.)
  - `Concentration in food`
  - `Age at treatment initiation`

* The main ITP page has a section titled "Compounds In Testing" which states: "An introduction to the logic and rationale for the ITP can be found in Warner, et al... Provide a rationale for the choice of intervention" (this is instructions for submitters, not rationales for the compounds already selected).

* There are **no specific rationales** given for choosing the compounds listed in these archived pages.
